About Me

I am Arctic452910, or ArcticMusicProductions. I am a type 1 diabetic living in Alabama. I work primarily on Gamejolt. I LOVE retro music and love to make it, along with music that is a hybrid of both. I love the works of Toby Fox in UNDERTALE and DELTARUNE, and also like music from EarthBound, EarthBound Beginnings, Ducktales (NES), Mario, and my own games of course! If you want to learn more, I've got an ask box here on this site, as well as comment section!
